# 🚚 Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze supply chain performance, sales, profitability, shipping efficiency, and product performance.

---

## 📌 Business Problem

Businesses often face challenges in managing supply chain operations efficiently. This dashboard helps identify sales trends, profitable markets, shipping performance, delivery risks, and top-performing products to support better business decisions.

---

## 📂 Dataset

- Dataset: DataCo Supply Chain Dataset
- Format: CSV
- Tool Used: Microsoft Power BI
- Domain: Supply Chain & Logistics

## 💡 Business Insights

- Technology products generated the highest sales.
- Some markets contributed significantly more profit than others.
- Standard shipping mode handled the majority of deliveries.
- A small number of products contributed a large share of revenue.
- Late deliveries were concentrated in specific shipping modes.
- Higher discounts did not always result in higher profits.

---

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

- Focus inventory on high-performing product categories.
- Reduce dependency on low-profit products.
- Optimize shipping methods to minimize delivery delays.
- Review discount strategies to improve profitability.
- Improve logistics performance in high-risk delivery regions.
